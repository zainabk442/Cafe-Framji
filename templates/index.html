Our story

Three generations, one corner of Fort.

Framji Irani opened the doors in 1962 with four marble tables and a wood-fired oven that still bakes the bread today. His grandson runs the counter now, though the mirrors on the wall, the glass jars of Osmania biscuits, and the "no outside food" plaque haven't moved an inch.

We're not a café that reinvents itself every season. Come for the kadak chai, stay because someone will eventually start talking to you about cricket.
